# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
delegated: dependency-free JavaScript modules with a small local Node.js server, chosen for direct browser play and offline runtime assets.

## Users
Players looking for a relaxed, short-session puzzle game on desktop, tablet, or phone.

## Product Purpose
Help restore the colorful glass windows of a small old-street workshop through a complete 36-level match-three story and a short replayable score challenge.

## Positioning
Handmade workshop craft and a warm neighborhood story shape the chapter progression. The match pieces now use five friendly animal faces, making the board easy to read and inviting to play.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Adjacent swaps, cascades, four-match beams, T/L-match lamp crystals, five-match lenses, special combinations, and board reshuffling.
- Three chapters of twelve authored stages, a fixed-layout side challenge, stars, workshop repairs, decorations, and a complete ending.
- Local save data only; no remote assets, accounts, payments, or energy timers.
- All confirmed visual and accessibility constraints are recorded in the original project brief supplied in the conversation.

## Brand Commitments
The Chinese title is 《流光灯铺》. The setting is a small glass-light workshop on an old street. The tone is warm, finely crafted, calm, and lightly narrative.

The user chose a midnight-blue night-street palette with warm golden window light for the page backgrounds and UI. The user later explicitly requested replacing the five pieces with round cartoon cat, rabbit, bear, fox and penguin faces. Preserve the existing board and page layouts, rules, special effects and progression. The user explicitly declined AI-generated imagery; use local CSS and authored vector assets.

The subsequent responsive task authorizes compact-layout repairs while preserving that visual identity and game progress. Prioritize a full visible board, goals and common actions on phones. Recommend portrait based on the square board's usable size, retain landscape play, and avoid forced orientation or restart.

## Evidence on Hand
No user-supplied image, sound, or brand files were present. The interface artwork and short sound cues are authored in the project.

## Product Principles
- Let the board stay readable while animal pieces remain distinct by silhouette, face and color. Keep special direction and type legible without hiding the animal's face.
- Teach rules through reachable levels and brief prompts.
- Keep retries immediate and progression local.
- Make the neighborhood story advance alongside workshop repairs.
